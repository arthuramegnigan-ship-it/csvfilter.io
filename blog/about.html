About CSVFilter.io

About the project

CSVFilter.io is a small, focused tool that does one thing well: it lets you open a CSV file in your browser, filter it down to the rows you actually need, and export the result — without uploading your data anywhere.

Why it exists

Working with exported data shouldn't mean wrestling with a heavy spreadsheet program every time. Most people who deal with CSVs — in marketing, HR, operations, analytics — just need to find the right rows and get them out. CSVFilter.io was built to make that specific job fast, visual and private.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. When you drop in a file, it's read and processed locally on your own device using JavaScript — the contents are never transmitted to a server. That means you can use it with internal contact lists, employee exports or any other data you'd rather keep on your machine.

From there you can search across all columns, filter by city or any specific field, keep only rows that contain an email, sort, hide columns you don't need, and export your filtered view as a clean CSV or a formatted PDF.

Who's behind it

CSVFilter.io is built and maintained by Arthur Busi, an independent developer. It started as a tool to solve a real, recurring frustration with large and messy CSV exports, and it's grown from there. Feedback shapes what gets built next.

Free and Pro

The tool is free to use for filtering and viewing files up to 200 rows. A Pro plan (€4.99/month) removes the row limit and unlocks CSV and PDF export, and can be cancelled at any time. Payments are handled securely through Stripe.
