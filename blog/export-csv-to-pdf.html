How to Export a Filtered CSV to a Clean PDF

Tutorial · 5 min read

A CSV is perfect for machines and awful for sharing. Send one to a colleague and they have to open it in something, fix the columns, and squint. Sometimes you don't want a spreadsheet at all — you want a clean, printable report with just the right rows. Here's how to go from a raw CSV to a tidy PDF in a couple of clicks.

Why turn a CSV into a PDF?

  • It's shareable. A PDF looks the same on every device — no broken columns, no "which app do I open this in?"
  • It's a snapshot. A report of this filtered view, on this date — useful for records, approvals or sending to someone who doesn't work in spreadsheets.
  • It's tidy. Clean headers, aligned columns, alternating row shading — readable at a glance.

The key step: filter first

The trick to a good report is exporting only the rows that matter, not the whole file. Filter the data down before you export, so the PDF contains your slice and nothing else.

  1. Import your CSV. Drag the file onto CSVFilter.io.
  2. Filter it down. Use global search, the city filter, a column filter, or the "with email" toggle. Stack them until the table shows exactly what you want.
  3. Trim the columns. Open the column picker and hide any fields that don't belong in the report.
  4. Sort it. Click a header so the rows come out in a sensible order.

Export to PDF

Click the PDF button. CSVFilter.io generates a formatted, landscape A4 document: a titled header, your column names across the top, and your filtered rows laid out with light alternating shading. It downloads straight to your device, ready to send or print.

Heads up. The PDF export covers up to 500 rows — ideal for a readable report. PDF and CSV export are Pro features; filtering and viewing are free.
